# House Blend

Medium roast blend of Colombian and Brazilian beans. Tasting notes: nutty, chocolate, mild acidity, smooth balanced body. Caffeine content: standard. Our most popular everyday drinker — designed to be forgiving across brew methods (drip, pour-over, French press, and standard drip machines all work well). This is the default bean in our Starter subscription plan.
