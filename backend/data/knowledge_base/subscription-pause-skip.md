# Managing Your Subscription

**Pausing**: you can pause a subscription for up to 3 months at a time. Paused subscriptions do not bill and do not ship until resumed. Resuming is instant and takes effect on the next scheduled cycle.

**Skipping a single shipment**: available up to 3 days before the scheduled ship date. Skipping does not cancel the subscription — it simply delays the next charge/shipment by one cycle.

**Switching plans** (e.g. Starter to Enthusiast): takes effect on the next billing cycle, not immediately. Mid-cycle switches are not supported to avoid partial-period billing complications.

**Cancelling**: can be done anytime with no fee. If cancelled less than 3 days before a scheduled shipment, that shipment may still go out and be charged — cancellation stops future cycles, not one already in the shipping window.
