# Subscription Plans

Three tiers:
- **Starter**: 1 bag (12oz) every 4 weeks, $16/bag, House Blend default (swappable).
- **Regular**: 1 bag (12oz) every 2 weeks, $15/bag (5% discount), any roast selectable.
- **Enthusiast**: 2 bags (12oz each) every 2 weeks, $14/bag (12% discount), any roast selectable, free shipping included.

All plans: cancel anytime, no contract lock-in. Roast selection can be changed for any upcoming shipment up until 3 days before it ships. Gift subscriptions available as a one-time 3, 6, or 12-month prepaid gift — these do not auto-renew.
