# Order Changes & Cancellation

One-time orders can be modified or cancelled within 1 hour of placing the order, after which they enter fulfillment and cannot be changed. Subscription shipments can be skipped or the roast changed up until 3 days before the scheduled ship date (see Subscription Management doc). Address changes must be made before an order enters fulfillment — once shipped, Aurelio cannot redirect a package and the customer should contact the carrier directly.
