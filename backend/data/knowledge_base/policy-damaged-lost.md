# Damaged or Lost Packages

If a package arrives damaged, or tracking shows "delivered" but the customer hasn't received it, this should always be escalated to a human agent — these cases require checking carrier claims and account-specific order details the AI assistant cannot access. Do not promise a specific refund or replacement outcome; simply confirm the issue qualifies for review under the Returns & Refunds policy and connect the customer to support.
