# General FAQ

**How long does coffee stay fresh?** Best within 4-6 weeks of the roast date printed on the bag. Store in a cool, dark place in the original resealable bag — avoid the fridge/freezer, which introduces moisture.

**Can I get a gift subscription?** Yes, prepaid gift subscriptions (3/6/12 months) are available and do not require the recipient to enter payment info.

**Do you offer decaf?** Yes, one decaf option (Colombia, Swiss Water Process) — see product docs.

**Can I mix roasts in one subscription shipment?** Not currently — each shipment is a single roast selection, but you can change your roast selection for future shipments anytime before the 3-day cutoff.

**Is your packaging recyclable?** Bags are made from a multi-layer film that is not curbside recyclable in most areas; we offer a mail-back recycling program via TerraCycle for an additional $2/order.
