# Sourcing & Sustainability

Aurelio pays a minimum of 20% above Fair Trade base pricing to partner farms. We work with fewer than 15 farms total across our three origin countries, prioritizing long-term direct relationships over broad sourcing networks. We are not currently Certified Organic or Fair Trade Certified (certification costs are prohibitive for our farm partners' scale), though our direct-trade pricing model is designed to exceed those certification standards in practice.
