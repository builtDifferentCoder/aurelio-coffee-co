# Ethiopia Yirgacheffe

Light roast, single origin from the Yirgacheffe region. Tasting notes: floral, jasmine, lemon citrus, light tea-like body. Caffeine content: standard (moderate-high, similar to most light roasts). Best brewed as pour-over or Chemex to highlight clarity. Not recommended for espresso — the delicate acidity gets lost under pressure brewing. Origin story: sourced from smallholder farms at 1,900-2,200m elevation, washed processing method.
