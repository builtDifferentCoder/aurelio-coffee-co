# Shipping

Domestic (US): standard shipping 3-5 business days, $4.95 flat rate, free on Enthusiast plan and on orders over $40. Expedited (2-day) available at checkout for $12.95.

International: currently ships to Canada and UK only. Canada: 7-10 business days, $14.95. UK: 8-12 business days, $18.95. Duties/customs fees are the customer's responsibility and not included in shipping cost.

Orders ship Monday-Thursday to avoid weekend transit delays for a perishable product. An order placed Friday-Sunday ships the following Monday.
