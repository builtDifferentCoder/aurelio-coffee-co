# Returns & Refunds

**Qualifies for refund/replacement**:
- Damaged packaging on arrival
- Wrong item shipped
- Order not received within 10 business days of shipment (domestic) or 21 days (international)
- Quality issue reported within 14 days of delivery

**Does not qualify**:
- Change of mind on roast/flavor after opening the bag
- Requests made more than 30 days after delivery
- Subscription shipments already in transit at time of cancellation request

**Process**: refunds and replacements are processed by a support specialist, not automatically issued — any refund/replacement request should be handed off to a human agent for verification, since it involves account and payment details the AI assistant does not have access to. Typical resolution time: 1-2 business days once escalated.
