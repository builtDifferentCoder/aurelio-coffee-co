# Pour-Over

Ratio: 1:16 (e.g. 20g coffee to 320g water). Grind: medium-fine, like table salt. Water temp: 195-205°F. Method: bloom with 2x coffee weight in water for 30-45 seconds, then pour remaining water in slow circular motions over 2.5-3.5 minutes total. Common mistake: pouring too fast, which under-extracts and gives sour, thin coffee. Best suited to light and medium roasts (Ethiopia Yirgacheffe, Colombia Huila, House Blend).
