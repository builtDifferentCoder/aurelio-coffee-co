# Colombia Huila

Medium roast, single origin from the Huila department. Tasting notes: caramel, red apple, balanced acidity, medium body. Caffeine content: standard. Versatile — works well as pour-over, drip, or French press. A good "everyday" roast for people who find light roasts too acidic. Origin story: family-owned farms at 1,600-1,900m, fully washed process.
