# Decaf

Medium roast Colombian decaf, processed using the Swiss Water Process (chemical-free decaffeination). Tasting notes: caramel, mild nuttiness, slightly muted acidity compared to its caffeinated counterpart. Caffeine content: 97%+ caffeine removed, trace amounts remain (~2-5mg per 8oz cup, versus ~95mg for regular coffee). Works with any brew method.
