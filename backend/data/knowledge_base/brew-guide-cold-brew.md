# Cold Brew

Ratio: 1:8 concentrate (e.g. 100g coffee to 800g cold water), dilute 1:1 with water or milk before drinking. Grind: coarse. Method: steep at room temperature or in the fridge for 12-18 hours, then strain through a fine mesh or cheesecloth. Common mistake: steeping longer than 24 hours, which introduces bitterness and over-extraction. Best suited to dark roasts (Sumatra Mandheling, Espresso Blend) — their low acidity holds up well to the long steep.
