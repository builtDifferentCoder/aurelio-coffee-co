# Espresso

Ratio: 1:2 (e.g. 18g dose to 36g output). Grind: fine, like powdered sugar. Water temp: 195-200°F. Method: 25-30 second extraction time for a double shot. Common mistake: grinding too coarse, which causes fast, sour, weak shots — if your shot pulls in under 20 seconds, grind finer. Use our Espresso Blend specifically; other roasts are not tuned for pressure extraction and will taste flat or overly bitter.
