# Sumatra Mandheling

Dark roast, single origin from North Sumatra. Tasting notes: earthy, low acidity, herbal, full body, notes of dark chocolate. Caffeine content: standard, though dark roasts often taste "stronger" despite similar caffeine to lighter roasts. Best brewed as French press or cold brew to match the heavy body. Processed using the traditional wet-hulling (giling basah) method, which gives it its distinct earthiness.
