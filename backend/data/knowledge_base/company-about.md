# About Aurelio

Aurelio Coffee Co. is a specialty coffee roaster founded in 2019, sourcing single-origin and blended coffees directly from small farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Indonesia. We roast in small batches weekly and ship fresh — every bag is roasted within 5 days of shipment. We're a subscription-first company: most of our business is recurring subscribers, with one-time orders also available.
