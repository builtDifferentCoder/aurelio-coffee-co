# Espresso Blend

Dark roast blend, formulated specifically for espresso extraction. Tasting notes: dark chocolate, toasted nut, low acidity, heavy crema. Caffeine content: standard per shot, though people often drink smaller volumes so total caffeine per serving can be lower than a large drip coffee. Not recommended for pour-over — the roast profile is tuned for pressure extraction and tastes flat/bitter when brewed as drip.
