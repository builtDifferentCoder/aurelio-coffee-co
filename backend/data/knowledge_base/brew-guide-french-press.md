# French Press

Ratio: 1:15 (e.g. 30g coffee to 450g water). Grind: coarse, like breadcrumbs. Water temp: 200°F. Method: add coffee and water, stir once, steep 4 minutes, then press slowly and pour immediately (leaving it sitting on the grounds after pressing over-extracts). Common mistake: using too fine a grind, which causes sediment and bitterness and can clog the filter mesh. Best suited to dark roasts (Sumatra Mandheling, House Blend).
